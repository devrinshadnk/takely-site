Takely: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Takely ("the app") is made by Rinshad Nadukkandi. This policy explains what happens to your information.

Your health data stays on your phone

The medications, schedules, doses, notes and photos you enter are stored only on your iPhone. They are never sent to us, and we have no server that receives them. Reminders are scheduled locally by iOS.

Backups and reports

If you export a backup or a report, the file goes only where you choose in the iOS share sheet.

Photos and camera

If you add a photo of a medication, it is stored inside the app on your device. Takely only accesses the camera or photo library when you choose to add a photo.

Purchases

Takely Pro is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details. To check whether you own Pro and to restore it, the app uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.), which receives your purchase history for this app and an anonymous identifier it creates. It does not receive your medications or any health data. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking
  • We don't sell or share personal or health data

Medical disclaimer

Takely is a reminder tool and does not provide medical advice.

Children

The app doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
