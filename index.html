Common questions

I'm not getting reminders. Check Settings › Notifications › Takely is allowed, and that Time Sensitive Notifications are on so reminders reach you in Focus modes.

I bought Takely Pro on another device. Open Settings in Takely and tap Restore Purchases with the same Apple ID.

Where is my data? Only on your iPhone. Use Settings › Backup to save a copy.

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Takely is a reminder tool and does not provide medical advice. Always follow your doctor's or pharmacist's instructions.
